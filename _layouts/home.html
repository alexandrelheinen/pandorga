Six full-bleed bands alternating on the surface ladder: hero, featured article, writing, rascunhos, systems, ideas graph. Every band renders from a field that already exists; the mockup's counters, reading times, reference codes and desk notes have no backing field and are not drawn. The tagline and author name lead the copy column. The name grows with that column up to the page-title step, and on a phone it does not drop below the band titles (Articles, Rascunhos, Projects). The portrait is a narrower plate stretched to the same height as the copy. The plate starts black, with no photograph. Each page load fades in one image from content/media/images/hero/ once that file has loaded (docs/features/hero-portraits.md). The three listing bands are filled in the browser from `_content_json/data/writing_index.json` by the shared blocks in `content-runtime/43-blocks.html`. Filter routes travel as data attributes so the script below stays free of Liquid. Every band introduces itself with a kicker, a title and a one-line note, and none of the three is written here: they are editorial copy, they belong to whoever writes the site rather than to whoever changes this layout, and they arrive at runtime from `content/pages/headers.yml` through `hydrateHomeSections()` in `content-runtime/60-fragments.html`. Each band names the entry it reads by page key and marks its slots; a field left empty in that file takes its element out of the page.
