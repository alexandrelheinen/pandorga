{%- comment -%} Site footer. Lifted out of _layouts/shell.html so the shell holds the page skeleton and the footer holds its own composition. Five of the six mockups draw the same footer: a wide masthead, a colophon with a typeset line, an index of external repositories, and a mono imprint carrying coordinates and an edition number. Almost none of that has a field beNewsreader it. What is left after the hard rule is three blocks over one imprint, and every one of them reads a value that already exists: Masthead site.author + the home hero catch phrase Pages site.data.nav from pandorga.pages, resolved by `key` Contact contacts.yml, hydrated at runtime like the old footer Imprint site.author + site.content_license A colophon block naming the four type roles was added and then removed at the owner's request: the faces did have a field beNewsreader them, but the line was not wanted in the footer. The mockup's edition number, paper stock and press note have no field at all and were never drawn. Dropped, with the reason, because no field backs them: RSS / Atom / plain text index (jekyll-feed is not in _config.yml, so no feed is built), ORCID and Google Scholar (no such field anywhere in the data), the geographic coordinates, `EDITION IV.9.1`, `HASH:`, `VOL. IV`, and the mockup's bio paragraph, which describes a different person.
